How to read it

  • Per game. Each team's totals are divided by the games it played in the window (full season or its last 4 games), so bye weeks don't count against it.
  • Points allowed adds up the fantasy points opposing QBs, RBs, WRs and TEs scored against each defense. Rank 1 allowed the most, which is the easiest matchup.
  • Your scoring. PPR or half PPR, passing TD points, TE premium and first-down bonuses all come from your league settings (the league button at the top). Yards: 0.04 per passing yard, 0.1 per rushing or receiving yard; 6 per rushing or receiving TD; −2 per interception or lost fumble; 2 per two-point conversion.
  • Source. nflverse weekly player stats (regular season), refreshed after each Tuesday update and on Wednesdays. Kickers and defensive scores are not included.
