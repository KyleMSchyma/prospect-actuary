Buy / Sell

After a player's first NFL season, how he was used (snaps, targets, touches, his share of the offense, and the late-season trend) says more about his next two years than his fantasy points did. These lists come from our year-2 and year-3 usage models, tested class by class on every drafted RB, WR and TE since 2010 with each class scored by a model that never saw it.

A sell does not mean the player is bad. It means his points ran ahead of the role behind them, so his price may be higher than what players with that usage usually go on to do. A buy is the reverse. "Chance of a starter season" is the model's chance of at least one top-24 RB, top-36 WR or top-18 TE season in the next two years. The lists were made after the 2025 season for the 2025 class (year 2 in 2026) and the 2024 class (year 3 in 2026); they are not updated during the season. Quarterbacks are not listed.
