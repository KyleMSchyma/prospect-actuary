          How to read it

          • Total value adds every player's value and, in dynasty, every valued draft pick. Values are on our 100,000-point scale, so they add up.
          • Starters now is the redraft value of the team's best possible starting lineup this week, using your league's lineup. Taxi-squad players are left out of it.
          • Outlook: the top third of teams on Starters now are Contenders, the bottom third Rebuilders, everyone else Middle. A rebuilder with a big dynasty total is set up for later.
          • Draft picks: a 2027 pick is valued at its exact slot once your league sets the 2027 draft order. Until then it gets the average value of its round. Our values cover rounds 1 to 3.
          • Needs compare each team's starters at a position with the rest of the league, in the mode you picked. Two ways to measure: Starters counts a team's best players for the starting spots an average team uses at that position, flex and superflex included; Whole group counts every valued player there, depth included. Bottom third = need, top third = strength. Trade partners are teams strong where you're weak, ranked higher when they're weak where you're strong.
          • Contending and rebuild moves are one-for-one swaps with another team in the league where both dynasty values are within 10% (too close to call, so a fair ask). A contending move gets back more than 10% more redraft value, a rebuild move more than 10% more future value (the 2027-2031 part of dynasty value; a 2027 pick is all future). Contenders see contending moves and rebuilders see rebuild moves until you pick one. See every move.
          • Players we don't value yet (very deep backups, a few new signings) show a value of 0 and are counted on the roster as "not valued".
