How it works

Prospect Actuary uses historical production, draft information, opportunity, age, availability, and points over replacement. Every major model is tested against future or held-out results, and known limitations are published.

How the Models WorkView Track RecordData and Limitations
  • Chances, not verdictsGrades come with the odds of each fantasy tier.
  • Credibility weightingThis season's games are blended with history by how much they can be trusted.
  • Survival and agingFuture seasons count by the chance a player is still producing.
  • Present valueLater seasons are discounted, so values add like money.
