What to take from the grades

  • A "hit" means at least one top-tier fantasy season (top 24 at QB and RB, top 18 at TE, top 36 at WR) in a player's first three NFL years.
  • Pre-draft A-range players hit about 59% of the time, B about 31%, C and below about 14%. Post-draft A-range players hit 69%, and D/F players almost never.
  • Every historical grade comes from a model that never saw that draft class, so the hit rates are honest out-of-sample results.
  • The draft pick still knows more than college stats alone. Treat the grade as a strong flag, not a guarantee.
