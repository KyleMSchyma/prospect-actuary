Contending and rebuild moves

Fair one-for-one swaps that keep your dynasty value level and move it to when you need it. Contending moves trade for players worth more this season. Rebuild moves trade for players and picks worth more in 2027 and later.

HOW A MOVE QUALIFIESThe two dynasty values are within 10% of each other, so the trade is too close to call and a fair ask. The side you get is worth more than 10% more in redraft value (contending) or future value (rebuild). Values are estimates, so smaller gains are left out.

How to read it

  • Redraft value is what a player is worth for the rest of the 2026 season. Future value is the part of his dynasty value that comes from 2027 to 2031 (dynasty value minus its rest-of-2026 part). A 2027 rookie pick is all future value.
  • Gain is how much more redraft value (contending) or future value (rebuild) you get back. Dynasty change is what the swap does to your dynasty total. It is always within the 10% band.
  • On the board each player you give appears once, with his best match. "Moves for one player" lists every match for that player.
  • These are one-for-one swaps on value alone. Check that the player you get would start for you, and that the other team needs what you send. The league page shows each team's needs.
  • All values use the league saved in this browser. Change it with the League button at the top.
