          How to read it

          • Values add. Every player is on one 100,000-point scale built from fantasy points per game over replacement, so two players together are worth the sum of their values. A trade is fair when the totals are close.
          • Too close to call. The values are estimates, not exact prices. When the two totals are within 10% of each other, the calculator does not name a winner. The 10% band is a rule of thumb taken from how far the values have missed in backtests.
          • There is no "consolidation" bonus for the side getting the single best player. Roster spots matter in deep leagues, so if you are giving up depth you can't replace, lean a little toward the side with the best player.
          • Redraft counts the rest of the 2026 season only. Dynasty adds 2027-2031 with draft-slot potential, aging and the chance of staying in the league. Rookie picks only count in dynasty.
          • A value of 0 means the player is not expected to beat a replacement-level starter in your league over that window.
          • 2027 rookie picks: players in our 2027 mock draft use the post-draft model with the mock pick as draft capital, everyone else the pre-draft model. Pick 1.01 is worth the best 2027 prospect for your league, 1.02 the second best, and so on. How every value is built is in the guide.
