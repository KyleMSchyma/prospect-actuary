Track Record

Every grade on this site was tested on past draft classes, but those classes were also used to choose the models, so their scores flatter us a little. The 2024, 2025 and 2026 classes were never used for any choice. Their predictions were frozen on 2026-11-13 (pre-draft and post-draft probabilities, grades and values, with a hash so they cannot be edited) and are scored here against what actually happened. This is the true holdout.

Read with care: the samples are still small. The 2024 class has played two of its three seasons, the 2025 class one, the 2026 class none that are complete. "Hit" means at least one top-tier fantasy season in years 1-3 (QB, RB top 24; WR top 36; TE top 18), so rates will rise as more seasons are played. Results run through the 2025 season; scored on 2026-11-13.
