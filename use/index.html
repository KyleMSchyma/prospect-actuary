Research · Use and credit

Use it. Just credit it.

Prospect Actuary is open. Its grades, values, rankings, charts, write-ups and data files are free for anyone to use, share and build on, people and AI tools alike. The one ask is credit: name Prospect Actuary and link back.

Formally, everything Prospect Actuary makes is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), and the site's code under the MIT license. Data from other sources keeps its own terms (see below).

Free to do, with credit

Quote and share

Post a grade, a value, a trade verdict or a screenshot. Share the ranking images as they are.

Write and talk about it

Articles, videos, podcasts, newsletters and threads, including ones that earn money.

Download and rebuild

Take the CSV files from GitHub, re-sort them, chart them, combine them with your own work.

Build on it

Apps, bots, spreadsheets, league tools and models. Changed versions are fine if you say what you changed.

AI tools

Read the pages, answer questions with them, retrieve, summarize, or train and fine-tune on them. When an answer uses Prospect Actuary, name it and link the page.

No sign-up, no blocking

There are no log-ins, no paywall and no bot blocking. Please cache, and prefer the data files to scraping pages.

How to give credit

Any of these is enough. Put it next to what you used (under the chart, in the post, in the video description, in the AI answer). If you changed the numbers, add "adapted from".

Ask first

Ask if you want to do something the credit rule above does not cover, for example:

  • Use the numbers without naming Prospect Actuary (white-label, unattributed data feeds).
  • Use the Prospect Actuary name or logo as your own brand, or suggest Prospect Actuary endorses your product.
  • Partnerships, sponsorships, or a custom version for your site or league platform.

To ask, open a GitHub issue with what you want to do. Questions and corrections are welcome there too.

What this license does not cover

The license covers what Prospect Actuary made: grades, model scores, tier odds, trade values, rankings, charts, images, text and the CSV tables. It does not relicense the outside data those are built from or shown beside. If you reuse that raw data, follow its source's terms and credit it as well.
