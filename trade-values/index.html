These values add up

Every value is on one 100,000-point scale, and values add. Two players together are worth the sum of their values, so a 60,000 player plus a 40,000 player equals one 100,000 player. To check a trade, add up each side and compare the totals.

The values are built from fantasy points per game over replacement: how many more points a player scores each game than the player you could start instead. Who that is depends on your league, so set your league below and every value updates. Because the values are measured in points, they add up the way points do. The top player on each sheet is 100,000.

Redraft Trade Values

Value for the rest of the 2026 season only: points per game over replacement × the games a player is expected to play from here. Points per game blend this season with his recent seasons, and expected games account for his history of missed games.

Dynasty Trade Values

Value over the rest of 2026 and the next four seasons. It adds a young player's potential from his draft slot, how players at his position age, his chance of staying in the league, and a discount that makes later seasons count a little less.

2027 rookie picks are priced from the pre-draft model, with no NFL draft capital, until the 2027 class is drafted. Each 2027 prospect gets an expected value from his chances of each fantasy tier, with what past players in that tier scored, adjusted for his age. Then the prospects are ranked for your league: pick 1.01 is worth the best prospect's value, 1.02 the second best, and so on through round 3. It assumes every prospect on the board enters the draft.

Read them with care

  • 2026 data runs through the latest week shown above. Values will move as the season goes on.
  • Each scoring setting has its own fitted model (credibility, aging, availability and staying in the league), not just rescored points. Lineup settings move the replacement lines.
  • QB catches always score full PPR, and RB or WR passing TDs always score 4 points. Both are too rare to matter. There is no first-down bonus.
  • "Games missed" counts any game without a stat line, so a benching looks like an injury. The injury report is not used yet.
  • Rookie pick values use college data only, so they are rougher than player values. NFL draft slot is the strongest signal for rookies, and it isn't in yet. Ages are estimated from years out of high school.
  • In backtests on 2016-2025, the redraft values and the dynasty values both ranked what actually happened better than "his current pace continues," for every scoring setting and every league size tested. Details are in the guide.
