# Third-party market data: not covered by the Prospect Actuary license

- `dd.js` and `tm.js` hold market values from [Dynasty Daddy](https://dynasty-daddy.com) (its public values API and,
  for `tm.js`, aggregates from its Trade Database, used with the owner's permission). They are **not** licensed under
  CC BY 4.0 by Prospect Actuary. Ask Dynasty Daddy before reusing them.
- `dp.js` holds a saved copy of [DynastyProcess](https://github.com/dynastyprocess/data) open values; follow that
  repository's license.

Our own values and the rank differences we compute are Prospect Actuary's work (CC BY 4.0, see
https://kylemschyma.github.io/prospect-actuary/use/).
