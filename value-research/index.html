      How to read it

      • Our side is the Trade Values page set to a 12-team superflex league with 0.5 PPR, 4-point passing TDs, and 2 RB, 3 WR, 1 TE starters, no flex. Dynasty projects fantasy points per game over replacement for the rest of 2026 through 2030; redraft covers the rest of 2026 only.
      • Dynasty Daddy values come from its public values API (superflex trade values, dynasty and redraft), saved with the site and refreshed each week rather than pulled on every visit.
      • DynastyProcess values are its superflex value (value_2qb), which turns FantasyPros expert consensus dynasty rankings into trade values.
      • Neither market source changes with your scoring settings, so small gaps can come from scoring alone.
      • Rookie picks are left out, since our pick values come from the pre-draft model and theirs from a different scale.
      • A big gap isn't a mistake on either side. It flags a player worth a closer look: our model may see production the market hasn't priced in, or the market may know something (role change, injury news, draft pedigree) the numbers don't capture yet.
